Contextual reminders and living widgets.

1. REMINDERS

The daily reminder adapts to what actually happened, and never guilt-trips.

- On a day you already prayed: no reminder at all. Pray once during the day,
  then check that nothing fires in the evening.
- For the two days after a prayer where you picked an emotion: the reminder
  offers a verse on that theme. It must never name the emotion nor point you
  back to how you felt.
- On Sunday, if an open intention is more than seven days old: an invitation
  to come back to it. The intention text must NEVER appear on the lock
  screen. Tapping it opens the Intentions screen.
- Any other day: the usual reflection question.

2. THE "MY VERSE" WIDGET

- It must never be empty nor stay stuck on an old verse.
- On the day you pick an emotion it shows that emotion's verse; on the
  following days a verse of the day takes over.
- "Another verse" button: the verse changes in place, without opening the
  app and without a wait.
- Switch the iPhone language between French and English: the widget verse
  must follow (Louis Segond 1910 / Berean Standard Bible).

3. THE "PRAY" CONTROL

- Add it to Control Center, and assign it to the Action button if your
  iPhone has one.
- It must open free prayer directly, without going through the home screen.
- Test it with the device locked too.

4. WHAT MUST NOT HAVE CHANGED

The "Pray" widget and the older shortcuts still open the app in the right
place. Journal, stats and guided prayer are untouched.

Thank you!
