HolyDay is a daily prayer journal, built to help you pray freely — without guilt.

Each day, take a moment for yourself:

• Mood check-in — simply share how you're feeling today.
• A Bible verse that meets your mood — from the Berean Standard Bible, chosen to speak to what you're going through.
• ACTS guided prayer or free prayer — Adoration, Confession, Thanksgiving, Supplication… or simply your own words.
• Prayer journal — keep track of your prayers and look back on the journey.

No streak to keep alive, no chain to break, no pressure. Pray whenever you want, at your own pace.

No account, no sign-in. Everything is available right away.

Donations are entirely optional and never unlock any feature.
